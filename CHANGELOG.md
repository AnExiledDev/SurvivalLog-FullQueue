# Changelog

## 0.3.0

- The Mods screen links this changelog.
- On the Mods screen, settings that only matter while another setting is on stay hidden until it is.
- QueueInPrologue and QueueOnExpeditions can be changed on the Mod Manager's Mods screen without a restart: the first applies the next time a game loads, the second from the next expedition. Enabled and ScrollQueue take effect at the next game start.
- The Mods screen shows the mod's name, description, category and links.
- Exiled's Toolkit 0.4.0 or newer is optional. With it, settings changed on the Mods screen take effect without a restart; without it, at the next game start.

## 0.2.1

First public release. Queue up several orders in the countdown at the start of a game and on expeditions, the same way you can at home, instead of one at a time.
