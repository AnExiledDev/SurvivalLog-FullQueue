# Changelog

## 0.3.1

- Fixed: the count of orders out of sight (such as +3) is back on the last slot of the queue bar instead of the third.
- Fixed: all five slots you can see take clicks, and each mouse wheel step moves exactly one slot.

- After a game update, Mod Manager can tell you if this mod may need an update.

## 0.3.0

- Mod Manager's Mods screen links to this changelog.
- On the Mods screen, a setting that only matters while another one is on stays hidden until that one is on.
- QueueInPrologue and QueueOnExpeditions can be changed on the Mods screen without a restart. QueueInPrologue applies the next time you load a game, and QueueOnExpeditions from your next expedition. Enabled and ScrollQueue take effect the next time you start the game.
- Mod Manager's Mods screen shows the mod's name, description, category and links.
- Exiled's Toolkit 0.4.0 or newer is optional. With it, settings you change on the Mods screen take effect right away. Without it, they take effect the next time you start the game.

## 0.2.1

- First public release.
- Queue up several orders in the countdown before the disaster and on expeditions, the same way you can at home.
