## 0.4.0

- The countdown before the disaster now has a queue bar. The game doesn't show one there, so the mod adds one in the bottom left corner. The buttons there move up to make room for it.
- The countdown queue bar looks like the normal one. A big box shows what your character is doing right now, with a ring showing how far along it is. When your character is storing items, the big box also shows how many are put away. The waiting orders come after the big box.
- Click an order or the big box in the countdown queue bar to cancel it. The bar goes away when the disaster starts.
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.20151.

## 0.3.2

- The game runs smoother with the scrolling queue bar. The mod does less work in the background and only updates the bar when your queue changes.
- The mod writes its log file in the background, so writing to it can't slow the game down.
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.19611.

## 0.3.1

- Fixed: the number of orders out of sight (such as +3) shows on the last slot of the queue bar again instead of the third.
- Fixed: clicking works on all five slots you can see.
- Fixed: each step of the mouse wheel scrolls exactly one slot.
- After a game update, Mod Manager can tell you if this mod may need an update.

## 0.3.0

- Mod Manager's Mods screen links to this changelog.
- On the Mods screen, the settings that only matter while `Enabled` is on are hidden while it's off.
- QueueInPrologue and QueueOnExpeditions can be changed on the Mods screen without a restart. QueueInPrologue applies the next time you load a game, and QueueOnExpeditions applies from your next expedition. Enabled and ScrollQueue take effect the next time you start the game.
- Mod Manager's Mods screen shows the mod's name, description, category and links.
- Exiled's Toolkit 0.4.0 or newer is optional. With it, settings you change on the Mods screen take effect right away. Without it, they take effect the next time you start the game.

## 0.2.1

- First public release.
- Queue up several orders in the countdown before the disaster and on expeditions, the same way you can at home.
